# Design decisions

The log of paper 2's design decisions (D1-D19) and of its dated amendments, for reviewers who check what was fixed before which result was seen, and for anyone proposing a change ([AGENTS.md](../../AGENTS.md) rule 11). Each decision gives what was decided, with its exact values, the alternatives considered, why, the evidence and the specification that holds the rule. The specifications ([RESEARCH.md](RESEARCH.md), [SIMULATION.md](SIMULATION.md), [BENCHMARK.md](BENCHMARK.md)) are the source of truth for each rule; this file records why. The decisions were drafted from the study lead's decisions memo (revision 2, 2026-10-28), a planning file kept with the project's research notes outside this repository. Where the memo was silent, this record decides in its spirit and marks the text *Added here*.

D1-D19 were made on 2026-10-28 by an AI agent (Claude, Anthropic) acting as study lead under authority delegated by the project lead, Pranav Reddy, and are pending his review.

Status on 2026-10-28: no faulted-versus-clean outcome has been computed, and `protocol-v1` is not yet tagged. No clinician has reviewed any item. Clinical dependencies are marked **[clinical review CRn]** and wait for a clinician in [CLINICAL_REVIEW.md](../CLINICAL_REVIEW.md); Cohen Ferrari's literature fact-check is not clinical review. Values we chose are marked **[assumption]** with the reason. Literature keys are in [CITATIONS.md](../CITATIONS.md). "SPIKE §n" is a section of the harness record, [SPIKE_2026-10-28.md](../development/SPIKE_2026-10-28.md). "Our arithmetic" marks a value we derived from cited numbers.

CR numbers follow the order of the study lead's clinical list: CR1 the per-event SESOIs and their per-day reading; CR2 the person's wizard, rescue rule and night response; CR3 treating on the displayed CGM; CR4 the correcting-person rule; CR5 the settings rule and its acceptance criteria; CR6 risk-weighted minutes (GRI weights) as one harm scale; CR7 whether 12 h captures the harm; CR8 the "severe" and "stress" fault levels; CR9 the interference sizes and time courses; CR10 the gate policy, with the person still seeing the raw CGM.

### oref0 0.7.1 as shipped

- Low-glucose suspend: threshold = min_bg − 0.5 × (min_bg − 40), so 70 mg/dL at min_bg = 100 mg/dL [OpenAPS docs]. A predicted low sets a zero temp of 30-120 min (`determine-basal.js:906-927`). This is the main path by which a false low acts.
- Stale data: a reading more than 12 min old ends the cycle (`determine-basal.js:187`). Flat CGM: bg > 60 mg/dL with delta 0 and both average deltas below 1 mg/dL per 5 min blocks dosing (`determine-basal.js:177-185`), and the check is skipped when the device is `fakecgm`. In either case a running high temp becomes a neutral temp for 30 min and no SMB is given (`determine-basal.js:198-221`).
- Noise: noise 2 raises the target by 1.3, and noise 3 or more stops looping [oref0 PR 833]. These rules act only when the CGM sends a noise field.
- SMB is disabled when maxDelta exceeds 20% of BG or the guard BG is below the threshold (`determine-basal.js:862-879`). The CLI wrapper turns SMB off when there is no autotune directory (`bin/oref0-determine-basal.js:105-114`).
- max_iob defaults to 0 (`lib/profile/index.js:15`), which blocks every high temp.
- glucose-get-last keeps only readings from the newest reading's device (`lib/glucose-get-last.js:32`) and averages changes over 2.5-17.5 min and 17.5-42.5 min. A freeze therefore trips the flat check after about 15-20 min when glucose was already flat, but needs about 45 min when glucose was moving (our reading of the code, to be confirmed by the harness-validity suite, D12).
- In clean harness runs the flat branch fires on 3.6-5.6% of calls, because whole-mg/dL readings and smooth noise give delta 0 (SPIKE §3.6).

### The simulator and the harness

- simglucose 0.2.11 implements the 2008 UVA/Padova model, which lacks counter-regulation and intraday variability [Dalla Man 2014; Visentin 2018]. The FDA's acceptance of the UVA/Padova simulator concerns the commercial T1DMS versions, not simglucose [Kovatchev 2009].
- Untreated lows in this model run until the insulin wears off. Paper 1's first cohort spent 5.6% (default mix) and 20.6% (high mix) of time below 54 mg/dL, with median run nadirs of 22 and 0.5 mg/dL, so paper 1 added a rescue rule (its amendment A6) [Reddy 2026].
- Paper 1's realistic default puts a compression low on 86% of nights. That reproduces 0.321 compression lows per day reaching a CGM below 85 mg/dL [Lobo 2026], because most simulated compression lows start from higher overnight glucose [Reddy 2026]. With a compression low every night, compression caused 0.32 (95% CI 0.30-0.35) of the trend alarm's 1.53 (1.33-1.73) false alarms per day, about 21% [Reddy 2026].
- Harness checks already passing: all 52 determine-basal fixtures (oref0's 42 test calls and 10 for branches they never reach) give oref0's own result through the bridge; the CLIs agree; replies do not change when the wall clock moves to 2019 or 2034; oref0's 84 mocha tests pass (SPIKE §2.2). Online injection equals paper 1's post-hoc injection bit for bit on 55,702 of 55,702 readings (SPIKE §4). The open loop with paper 1's bolus rule reproduces paper 1's engine bit for bit (SPIKE §3.5). For each controller a branched clean arm equals the continuous run bit for bit; arms are bit-identical to the trunk before onset and share sensor noise and meals after it (SPIKE §5). The ODE runs at 0.56 s per simulated day (SPIKE §3.3).
- Settings: for adult#001 (3 days, standard scenario, calibration seed 90001, `oref0_tb`, no faults) the Quest CF of 8.77 mg/dL/U is a quarter of 1800/TDI, 35.7 mg/dL/U. With the Quest CF, time below 70 mg/dL was 5.16% despite 119 low-glucose suspends; with 1800/TDI it was 0.00%, with time in range 95.8% (SPIKE §3.6). This is one patient in a harness check, not the selection run of D4.
- Not yet done: the 30-patient calibration and the timing of a trunk and an arm (SPIKE §1).

### Fault sizes and rates

- Compression: median depth 24 mg/dL (5th-95th percentile 10-48 mg/dL) and median duration 45 min (30-70 min) [Facchinetti 2016]. Published rates differ about tenfold: 0.03 per person-day [LoopEval 2026] to about 0.33 per day [Idi 2025].
- False highs from drugs: vitamin C on Libre 2 gave +9.3 mg/dL 2-3 h after 1000 mg, and 18.4 and 19.7 mg/dL after a second and third dose 4 h apart, independent of background glucose [FDA K193371]. Acetaminophen 1 g gave a largest mean difference of 61 mg/dL at 120 min on the Dexcom G4 [Maahs 2015; numbers from a secondary report], +3.1 mg/dL on the G6 [Calhoun 2018], and +55 to +114 mg/dL intravenously [Matsuyama 2025]; plasma glucose stayed flat while the CGM rose [Basu 2016]. Hydroxyurea labels name insulin over-delivery [Medtronic Guardian 4 UG; FDA K203774]; one report shows about +63 mg/dL [MAUDE 2026].
- Defective sensors: Class I recalls for Libre 3 false highs in 2024 (8,174 sensors, 2 injuries) and Libre 3/3 Plus false lows in 2025 (860 serious injuries and 7 deaths as of 2026-01-07) published no magnitude or time course [FDA 2024 Libre 3 false-high recall; FDA 2025 Libre 3 false-low alert].
- Gaps: G6 trial data give about 0.13 (adults) and 0.24 (children) gaps per day (our arithmetic), lasting typically 25-30 min and up to about 95 and 120 min. They are retrospective downloads, which may miss short gaps that were later backfilled [Vettoretti 2023]. Paper 1's short-gap model (2 gaps per day at s = 0.5, exponential length with 90% under 20 min [Facchinetti 2016]) gives P(gap > 12 min) = 0.25 and P(gap > 20 min) = 0.10 (our arithmetic), so it rarely reaches oref0's 12-min limit. Its rate has no source and is 8 to 30 times the G6 trial rate (our arithmetic).
- Frozen values: one field failure showed a stale value without alerts to 61,355 app users, for an unpublished time [openFDA recall 96684]. No rate or duration data exist.
- Real-world evidence of AID harm from CGM faults is recall-, label- and single-case-level. In one case a 10-year-old's compression false lows led to insulin suspension and rebound highs, and 12 of 14 nights needed intervention [Altnickel 2026].

### Outcomes and statistics

- Consensus targets: time in range 70-180 mg/dL above 70%, time below 70 mg/dL under 4% and below 54 mg/dL under 1%; a 5-percentage-point change in time in range is clinically meaningful [Battelino 2019]. A hypoglycaemic event lasts at least 15 min [Battelino 2023].
- GRI weights: 3.0 (below 54 mg/dL), 2.4 (54-69 mg/dL), 1.6 (above 250 mg/dL) and 0.8 (181-250 mg/dL) [Klonoff 2023].
- Equivalence by two one-sided tests (TOST) [Lakens 2017]; simulation studies reported in ADEMP form [Morris 2019]; credibility evidence proportionate to model risk [ASME 2018; FDA 2023].
